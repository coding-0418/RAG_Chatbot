# Kuvera Platform Guides (Official Help Articles)

Platform: Kuvera (https://kuvera.in)

## Download Mutual Fund Portfolio Holding Statement

Source: https://kuvera.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/82000909447-how-do-i-download-my-mutual-fund-portfolio-holding-statement-

Steps:
1. Log in to your Kuvera account.
2. Go to the Portfolio tab.
3. Click "Manage Funds" -> "Download Reports".
4. Choose the Excel or PDF icon to download portfolio holdings as of date.

## Download Capital Gains Report

Source: https://kuvera.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/82000725676-how-do-i-get-my-capital-gains-report-for-filing-taxes-

Steps:
1. Log in to your Kuvera account.
2. Navigate to the Reports section.
3. Select the financial year for which you need the capital gains statement.
4. Download the mutual fund capital gains report for tax filing.

## Tax Reports on Kuvera

Source: https://kuvera.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/82000725727-how-do-i-get-tax-reports-on-kuvera-

Available reports in the Reports section:
- ELSS (80C) Investments report by financial year.
- Capital Gains report by financial year.

## Download Transaction History

Source: https://kuvera.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/82000725654-how-do-i-download-my-mutual-fund-transaction-history-from-kuvera-

Steps:
1. After orders are processed, transactions appear in Reports.
2. Open the Reports section in your Kuvera account.
3. Download transaction history from there.
