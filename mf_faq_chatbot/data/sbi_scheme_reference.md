# SBI Mutual Fund Scheme Reference (Official Source Summary)

This local reference file supplements web ingestion with structured facts from SBI Mutual Fund SID/KIM/factsheet documents.
Source hub: https://www.sbimf.com/offer-document-sid-kim

## SBI Bluechip Fund (now SBI Large Cap Fund)

- Official scheme page: https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/sbi-large-cap-fund-(formerly-known-as-sbi-bluechip-fund)-43
- Category: Open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large cap stocks.
- Benchmark: S&P BSE 100 TRI (as per SID/KIM).
- Minimum SIP: Monthly SIP minimum is typically Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 1 thereafter (refer SID for current frequency-specific limits).
- Exit load and expense ratio: Disclosed in SID/KIM and on TER page https://www.sbimf.com/total-expense-ratio-of-mutual-fund-schemes
- Risk-o-meter: Published in factsheet and SID; equity large cap schemes are typically classified in higher risk categories per SEBI guidelines.

## SBI Contra Fund

- Official scheme page: https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/sbi-contra-fund-12
- Category: Open-ended equity scheme following a contrarian investment strategy.
- Investment objective: Long-term capital appreciation via diversified equity and equity-related securities using contrarian strategy.
- Minimum SIP: Monthly minimum Rs. 500 (or Rs. 1,000 per some frequencies) per SID/factsheet; verify latest SID.
- Exit load: Refer latest factsheet/SID for current exit load schedule.
- Benchmark and risk-o-meter: Disclosed in monthly factsheet at https://www.sbimf.com/factsheets

## SBI Long Term Equity Fund (ELSS / SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund)

- Official scheme page: https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/sbi-elss-tax-saver-fund-(formerly-known-as-sbi-long-term-equity-fund)-3
- Category: Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS).
- Lock-in period: Statutory lock-in of 3 years from date of allotment to avail Section 80C benefits.
- Minimum application amount: Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 50 thereafter (per SID).
- Tax benefit: Investments eligible for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to statutory limits.
- SID: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/lists/sid---sbi-elss-tax-saver-fund-(formerly-known-as-sbi-long-term-equity-fund).pdf

## SBI Magnum Midcap Fund

- SID: https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/lists/sid_kim/sid---sbi-magnum-midcap-fund.pdf
- Category: Open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in mid cap stocks.
- Investment objective: Long-term growth in capital by investing predominantly in a diversified basket of mid cap equity stocks.
- Asset allocation: Equity and equity related instruments of midcap companies 65%-100% (per KIM/SID).
- Minimum SIP, exit load, expense ratio, benchmark, risk-o-meter: Refer SID/KIM and factsheets.

## SBI Small Cap Fund

- Official scheme page: https://www.sbimf.com/sbimf-scheme-details/sbi-small-cap-fund-329
- Category: Open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in small cap stocks.
- Benchmark: BSE 250 Small Cap Index TRI (per factsheet).
- Exit load: 1% if redeemed within one year from allotment; Nil after one year (per published factsheet).
- SIP cap: Fresh SIP/STP registrations capped at Rs. 25,000 per month per PAN for monthly frequency (per scheme notices).
- Fresh lump sum subscriptions may be discontinued per AMC notices; verify latest SID/addendum.

## KIM and SID

- KIM (Key Information Memorandum): Condensed scheme information document provided to investors before investment.
- SID (Scheme Information Document): Detailed legal offer document with investment objective, asset allocation, fees, risks, and operational terms.
- Download portal: https://www.sbimf.com/offer-document-sid-kim
