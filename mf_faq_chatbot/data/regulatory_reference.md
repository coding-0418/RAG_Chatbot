# Regulatory Reference — SEBI and AMFI

## SEBI Risk-o-meter

Source: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html

- The Risk-o-meter is a mandatory pictorial risk disclosure tool for mutual fund schemes.
- SEBI requires AMCs to display the Risk-o-meter on scheme materials including factsheets.
- Risk levels include: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, and Very High.
- Fund houses must review and update risk levels periodically based on portfolio composition and market conditions.

SEBI circular on product labeling:
https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2020/circular-on-product-labeling-in-mutual-fund-schemes-risk-o-meter_47796.html

## SEBI Mutual Fund Investor FAQs

Source: https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/sep-2024/1727242783639.pdf

- NAVs must be published on AMC websites and on AMFI (www.amfiindia.com).
- SID, KIM, and portfolio details are available on AMC websites.
- Investors should use AMFI-registered distributors with valid ARN.
- Mutual funds are subject to market risks; read all scheme related documents carefully.

## AMFI Investor Education

Source: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor

- AMFI provides investor awareness programs and educational resources on mutual funds.
- AMFI content is informational and not sales promotion.
- Returns cannot be guaranteed; historical performance is not indicative of future results.
- How to invest: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/investor-center/how-to-invest.html
- KYC is mandatory before investing in mutual funds.
