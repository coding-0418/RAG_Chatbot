# Sample Q&A — Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant

Expected behavior for evaluation. Answers are grounded in approved sources; exact wording may vary slightly after RAG generation but must remain factual and include source citations.

---

## 1. Expense Ratio

**Question:** What is the expense ratio of SBI Bluechip Fund?

**Expected answer:** The expense ratio (Total Expense Ratio / TER) for SBI Bluechip Fund (now SBI Large Cap Fund) is published on SBI Mutual Fund's official TER disclosure page and updated periodically. Investors should refer to the latest TER table on sbimf.com for the current direct and regular plan figures.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/total-expense-ratio-of-mutual-fund-schemes

---

## 2. Exit Load

**Question:** What is the exit load for SBI Small Cap Fund?

**Expected answer:** Per the published SBI Small Cap Fund factsheet, exit load is 1% if units are redeemed within one year from the date of allotment, and Nil if redeemed after one year.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-small-cap-fund-factsheet-march-2026.pdf

---

## 3. Minimum SIP

**Question:** What is the minimum SIP amount for SBI Contra Fund?

**Expected answer:** The minimum SIP for SBI Contra Fund varies by frequency; monthly SIP minimum is typically Rs. 500 (or Rs. 1,000 for certain frequencies) in multiples of Rs. 1, as stated in the scheme SID/factsheet.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/lists/sid---sbi-contra-fund8ce474c1a5404a2b899f14f6f21a95dd.pdf

---

## 4. Benchmark

**Question:** What is the benchmark of SBI Small Cap Fund?

**Expected answer:** The first-tier benchmark for SBI Small Cap Fund is the BSE 250 Small Cap Index TRI, as disclosed in the official factsheet.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/sbi-small-cap-fund-factsheet-march-2026.pdf

---

## 5. Riskometer

**Question:** What is the Risk-o-meter for mutual fund schemes?

**Expected answer:** The Risk-o-meter is a SEBI-mandated pictorial tool that shows the risk level of a mutual fund scheme using categories such as Low, Moderate, High, and Very High. AMCs must display it on factsheets and update it periodically per SEBI guidelines.

**Source:** https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html

---

## 6. ELSS Lock-in

**Question:** What is the lock-in period of SBI Long Term Equity Fund?

**Expected answer:** SBI Long Term Equity Fund (SBI ELSS Tax Saver Fund) is an ELSS with a statutory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment to avail Section 80C tax benefits.

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/lists/sid---sbi-elss-tax-saver-fund-(formerly-known-as-sbi-long-term-equity-fund).pdf

---

## 7. KIM Explanation

**Question:** What is a KIM document for mutual funds?

**Expected answer:** A Key Information Memorandum (KIM) is a condensed official document published by the AMC that summarizes essential scheme information such as investment objective, asset allocation, fees, and risks. It complements the detailed Scheme Information Document (SID).

**Source:** https://www.sbimf.com/offer-document-sid-kim

**Question:** Is KYC required before investing in mutual funds?

**Expected answer:** Yes. KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory before investing in mutual funds, whether online or offline, as per AMFI investor guidance.
